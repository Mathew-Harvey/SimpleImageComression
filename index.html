🖼️ Local Bulk Image Compressor

Compress images to approximately 300KB each, directly in your browser

✨ How it works: All processing happens locally in your browser. No images are uploaded to any server.
⚠️ Important: For best results with large batches (100+ images), we process images sequentially to prevent browser crashes and ensure all images are compressed successfully.

📋 Usage Guidelines

  • Recommended: Process in batches of 50-100 images for optimal performance
  • Maximum tested: Up to 1000 images (may take significant time)
  • Processing speed: ~1-3 seconds per image depending on size and device
  • Browser limits: Keep browser tab active during processing
  • Memory usage: Large batches may use significant RAM
